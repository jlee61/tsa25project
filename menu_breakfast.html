Our Breakfast Items

Waffles

Apple cider vinegar mixed in silky soy milk, topped with maple syrup, vegan whipped cream, sweet, ripened strawberries and blueberries.

Pancakes

Smooth, rich vegan butter combined with non-dairy milk from our grass-fed cows raised on local farms. Freshly-plucked strawberries and blueberries can be added accordingly.

Omelettes

Pasteurized eggs, creamy butter, grated cheddar cheese, fresh baby spinach leaves, chopped red peppers and cherry tomatoes.

Oatmeal

Gluten free rolled oats, silky coconut milk, rich butter, coated in sweet maple syrup. Optional toppings include: ripe bananas and pears, plump berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries), crunchy apples.
